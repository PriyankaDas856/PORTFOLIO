# UX Challenges in Healthcare Technology

Building MedPass taught me that healthcare UX is a different beast. Here's what I learned.

## Trust is the Product

In consumer apps, trust is earned over time. In healthcare, you have seconds. Every pixel must communicate security, clarity, and competence.

## Design for Stress

Users might be sick, scared, or helping an elderly parent. Cognitive load must be minimal. One primary action per screen. Plain language always.

## Compliance Shapes UX

HIPAA isn't just backend — it affects what you show, when you show it, and how users consent. Design consent flows as carefully as core features.

## Accessibility is Non-Optional

Healthcare serves everyone. WCAG AA is the floor, not the ceiling. Test with screen readers. Test with one hand. Test tired at midnight.

## The Provider Perspective

Patients aren't your only users. Providers need efficiency — bulk actions, keyboard shortcuts, and integration with existing workflows.

Healthcare technology saves lives when it's usable. Design accordingly.
